How it plays

Simple enough for a coffee break, tricky enough to replay

Play solo against your own memory, or pass the phone back and forth for a two-player match.

1

Flip

Tap any two face-down cards to reveal what's underneath.

2

Match

Found a pair? It flies over to your score and you go again.

3

Miss

No match, no problem — the cards flip back and the turn passes on.

Browse & order

Every card doubles as something real

Open "Browse & Order" from the main menu to see the whole deck laid out like a catalog. Tap 💬 Order on WhatsApp and it opens a chat with your message already typed out — no forms, no accounts.

  • Search the catalog by name while you decide.
  • One tap starts a real WhatsApp conversation with us.
  • Works completely offline — ordering is the only part that needs a connection.
Always fresh

New cards, a few times a month

We swap in new pictures roughly three times a month. Updates quietly download in the background, so a game already in progress is never interrupted — you'll just see new cards next time you play.
